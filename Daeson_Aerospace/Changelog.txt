# __***Project: Showdown* Round 5 Update**__
Rounds 1-4 revealed multiple issues people were having with the current meta of PSC, and the imminent convergence of optimal design spurred radical new change for the competition. Hopefully, this will be for the better. Nearly every single part in PSC has been touched in some way. Some equipment has been overhauled completely. PSR has also received a major expansion, adding new nations with new parts to the comp. 

## __**General Updates**__

### __Dry Mass Update__
Missiles *NO LONGER* count towards Dry Mass. Remove your missiles before inputting Dry Mass to calculate LtW and RCS!!! You will be DQ'ed for having incorrect LtW. 

### __KSP Community Fixes__
A new version of KSPCF is included, supposedly boasting additional framerate gains.

# PS Patch 11-2-2024
An emergency hotfix to accomplish a couple things:

__BDA Global Damage Mult Fix__
For reasons unbeknownst to me, the global damage multiplier was set to 50. It has now been corrected to 150. 

__X3 Piercer Guided Shells__
The X3 Piercer has been given its guided shells, and correct damage numbers. Have fun!

__*Retro* Missile Guidance Mode__
Re-added ProNav guidance to all *Retro* missiles, except only as a terminal phase which allows them to achieve more optimal lead angles. (thanks to BillNye for the help)
