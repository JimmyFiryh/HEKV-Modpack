When compared with ordinance such as missiles, guns are exceedingly simple. A projectile uses its mass and velocity, sometimes combined with an explosive or incendiary payload, to damage a target. Guns must be aimed by the pilot and their rounds follow a ballistic arc to the target. They have two key advantages: no minimum effective or safe range, and no possibility of being interrupted during their flight to the target. The final defense of aircraft against missiles is countermeasures; the final defense of aircraft against guns is a pilot with bad aim.
