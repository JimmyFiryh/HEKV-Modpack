Semi-Active Radar Homing Air-to-Air Missiles use the launching aircraft's radar for locking and guidance. The launching aircraft must maintain a radar lock on the target until impact.

SARH missiles are generally the most reliable front-aspect weapon for use on alert targets at close range and all targets at medium ranges and beyond, at least when considering what was generally available during the mid-cold war period that this mod covers. They are your first option for shooting down targets, and will at the very least force them into evasion, granting you better relative positioning and maintaining your initiative. If you're so inclined, you could even utilize them during a dogfight should a good angle present itself. No one wants to see a sparrow rapidly approaching their face from a few hundred meters away.
