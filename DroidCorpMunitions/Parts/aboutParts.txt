/Missiles
//Missile ranges are set with a maximum value reflecting their theoretical engage range assuming absolute optimal conditions, i.e. you and your target are moving towards each other at high altitude and at a high closure rate. It also assumes you have dynamic range disabled. In almost all cases it is a good idea to shorten the engage range when placing these missiles on your craft according to your dynamic on/off setting choice and your use case. Likewise, gun ranges are calibrated for maximum reasonable engagement of static targets, specifically in a ground-attack role. Engage range against air targets should be lowered according to your expected accuracy and target maneuverability.

/Radars
//Radars in DCM include various models from the latest generation fighters all the way back to the earliest of 2nd gen. Some radars are more capable than others, of course, but crucially this includes varying feature sets. Some radars are physically incapable of even locking targets, while others are capable of locking multiple targets simultaneously. Scan sweep speeds also affect how often your craft receives updates on its tracks that are not being actively locked.
